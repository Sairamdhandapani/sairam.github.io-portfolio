Environmental intelligence / IoT

Scaling environmental intelligence through IoT

A multi-state technology programme to expand air-quality monitoring infrastructure and support access to more granular environmental data.

INR 6 crore programme IoT Implementation Government engagement
Explore the case study

The challenge

Expanding monitoring infrastructure across different geographies creates technical, operational and institutional coordination challenges. Monitoring systems need to be reliable and useful to the institutions expected to act on the evidence.

My contribution

I led the technology programme's implementation coordination, working with technical teams, implementation partners and government stakeholders. My responsibilities included deployment planning, field testing, sensor validation, operational troubleshooting and coordination across the programme ecosystem.

Implementation focus

  • Coordination across technical and institutional stakeholders.
  • Support for sensor deployment and field validation.
  • Operational troubleshooting and implementation follow-through.
  • Communication between technical teams and government partners.

Scale and learning

The programme involved more than 600 IoT sensors across six Indian states. It reinforced that monitoring infrastructure creates value not simply through data collection, but through system reliability and the ability of institutions to use evidence.

CASE STUDY 02 / CEEW
06
Cities · Hyperlocal pollution mapping
Environmental research / GIS

Making air pollution visible at the local level

Hyperlocal pollution-source mapping to build a geographically informed understanding of urban air pollution and support local action.

Air quality GIS Municipal engagement
Explore the case study

The challenge

Air pollution is influenced by numerous distributed sources. Broad city-level estimates alone may not provide municipalities with sufficiently local information to understand where interventions are needed.

My contribution

I contributed to research and municipal engagement related to ward-level mapping of dispersed air-pollution sources across six cities, helping translate environmental research into geographically relevant information.

Approach

  • Contributed to identification and categorisation of local pollution sources.
  • Supported organisation and interpretation of spatial environmental information.
  • Engaged with municipal stakeholders.
  • Helped translate technical findings into accessible research outputs and recommendations.

Relevance and learning

The work supported a more localised understanding of pollution sources and reinforced the importance of translating research into information that local institutions can understand and use.

CASE STUDY 04 / REAP BENEFIT
20%
Reported reduction in landfill waste
Waste management / Community action

Improving school waste management through practical intervention

A school-level waste-management initiative connecting environmental outcomes with operational savings.

Waste management Community action Measured outcomes
Explore the case study

The challenge

Without appropriate segregation and management practices, school waste can become part of the wider landfill burden. Institutions need practical systems that can be sustained in day-to-day operations.

My contribution

I contributed to a school-level waste-management initiative focused on improving waste handling and reducing landfill disposal.

Reported outcomes

The initiative reported a 20% reduction in landfill waste and generated annual savings exceeding INR 5 lakh.

A perspective on impact

Good environmental ideas matter. But systems make them scale.

Environmental progress depends on more than a promising technology or a well-written strategy. It requires credible evidence, appropriate funding, capable partners, implementation discipline and a clear understanding of outcomes.
