A little about me

Working at the intersection of evidence and impact.

I am a climate and environmental programme professional with experience across programme delivery, multi-stakeholder partnerships, applied research and environmental technology.

Currently, I lead coordination of a USD 6M+ multi-donor climate portfolio, engaging research organisations, government stakeholders, foundations, civil-society organisations and private-sector partners. Previously, I coordinated a INR 6 crore multi-state technology programme involving more than 600 IoT sensors across six Indian states.

My work spans programme strategy, partnership development, investment and delivery assessment, monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEL), environmental research and translating technical evidence into decision-ready recommendations.
