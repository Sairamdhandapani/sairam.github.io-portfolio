Case study 04 / Reap Benefit
20% less landfill waste

Improving school waste management through practical intervention

A school-level waste-management initiative connecting environmental outcomes with operational savings.

Waste managementCommunity actionMeasured outcomes
Explore the case study

The challenge

Without appropriate segregation and management practices, school waste can become part of the wider landfill burden. Institutions need practical systems that can be sustained in day-to-day operations.

My contribution

I contributed to a school-level waste-management initiative focused on improving waste handling and reducing landfill disposal.

Intervention focus

  • Supported the development of a practical waste-management intervention.
  • Considered waste segregation and resource recovery.
  • Contributed to implementation within a school environment.
  • Supported an approach linking environmental outcomes with operational savings.

Reported outcomes

The initiative achieved a reported 20% reduction in landfill waste and generated annual savings exceeding INR 5 lakh.

Learning and evidence to add

Show the baseline, measurement period, waste categories, calculation method and source of the savings figure. Add before-and-after photographs or a simple waste-flow diagram if available.

Current focus: climate programme and portfolio strategy

At AVPN, my work spans multi-donor climate portfolio coordination, programme assessment, partnership opportunities, funding discussions and monitoring, evaluation and learning. I am particularly interested in how funding, research and institutional collaboration can be organised to accelerate meaningful climate action.
