01 / Perspective

More than a job title.

My work sits at the intersection of climate action, environmental evidence, technology and people. Over the years, I have moved between research institutions, civic technology initiatives and cross-sector climate programmes.

I enjoy working on problems that require more than one discipline: understanding environmental systems, making technical information accessible, bringing stakeholders together and turning promising ideas into practical programmes.

Today, I coordinate a multi-donor climate portfolio and work with organisations across the climate ecosystem. My earlier experience includes environmental monitoring, IoT deployments, air-quality research, hyperlocal pollution source modelling, mapping and technology for civic and environmental problem-solving.
