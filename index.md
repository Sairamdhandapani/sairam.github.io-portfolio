## About

I am a climate and environmental programme professional with 9+ years of experience spanning programme delivery, multi-stakeholder partnerships, applied research and environmental technology.

My work sits at the intersection of evidence, strategy and implementation. I currently coordinate a USD 6M+ multi-donor climate portfolio, working with research organisations, government stakeholders, foundations, civil-society organisations and private-sector partners.

Previously, I coordinated a INR 6 crore, multi-state technology programme involving more than 600 IoT sensors across six Indian states. My experience includes programme and partnership development, investment and delivery assessment, monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEL), environmental research, and translating technical evidence into practical recommendations.

I am particularly interested in initiatives that turn environmental evidence and innovation into measurable, scalable impact.
